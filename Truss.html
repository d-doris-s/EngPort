Truss Design & Optimization

Designed and optimized a truss structure to maximize load capacity while maintaining a budget under $300. Using a MATLAB algorithm for force analysis, the final design held 60.58 oz of load (0.2794 oz/$), achieving a 79.3% increase in capacity compared to the initial prototype.
